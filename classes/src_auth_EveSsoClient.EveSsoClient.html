    Class EveSsoClient

    Minimal client for the EVE SSO OAuth2 endpoints: building the login URL, exchanging an authorization code, refreshing, and revoking.

    Supports confidential clients (HTTP Basic with the client secret) and public clients (PKCE with client_id in the body). No JWT signature verification is performed; tokens are trusted because they arrive directly from SSO over TLS.
